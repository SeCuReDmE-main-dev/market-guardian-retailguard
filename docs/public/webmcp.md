# RetailGuard WebMCP surface

RetailGuard exposes ten defensive education tools and the two SecuredMe companion tools through `src.webmcp`. The canonical slug is `retailguard`; legacy Market Guardian wording may remain descriptive but cannot create a second product identity.

All replays use synthetic fixtures. Results are evidence-review signals, never accusations, enforcement decisions, biometrics, or surveillance. Review cases and evidence entries are staged in ephemeral objects until a human authorizes an application-owned operation. Image detection fails closed when the approved local runtime is unavailable.

The companion's RetailGuard palette must come from the product-specific Stitch design source in the SecuredMe Education website asset catalogue, with accessible SecuredMe fallbacks.
